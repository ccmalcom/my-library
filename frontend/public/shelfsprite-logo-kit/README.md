# ShelfSprite logo kit

The logo pairs the existing ShelfSprite book-sprite mark with a custom outlined
wordmark based on **Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold**, the display typeface used by
the app. The outlined SVGs do not require the font to be installed.

## Files

- `shelfsprite-logo-dark.svg` and `.png` — warm-ivory wordmark for dark backgrounds.
- `shelfsprite-logo-light.svg` and `.png` — near-black wordmark for light backgrounds.

Both PNGs have transparent backgrounds. The SVG files are preferred for websites,
print, and any use where they can be supported without rasterization.

## Brand colors

- Warm black: `#161412`
- Coral: `#FF5C3A`
- Light coral: `#FF7355`
- Warm ivory: `#F5F0E8`

Keep clear space around the logo approximately equal to one of the sprite's eyes.
Do not abbreviate the name in the wordmark.
